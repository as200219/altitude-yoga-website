A practice
for being here.

Altitude is equal parts old-world loft and modern practice space, a place where the practice begins the moment you step through the door.

Our classes are designed to meet you at any level. Choose from unheated, warm, or hot rooms, with a pace that ranges from the slow stillness of yin to the strength and heat of power. Every class is led by an instructor who specializes in the discipline they teach, so you’re always in expert hands.

Arrive early. Stay a while. The quiet rituals before and after class are part of the practice here.
