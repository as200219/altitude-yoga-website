About Altitude Yoga

Made for
coming back to yourself.

A dedicated yoga studio close to home, above Align Pilates in the heart of Zebulon.

Our story

A place with
real character.

This studio exists because we wanted a dedicated yoga studio close to home.

Erica has practiced yoga for years. For Adam, a combat helicopter pilot who served multiple deployments overseas, it came later, a way to process what he had carried home, to breathe, to reset, to come all the way back.

What they both love about yoga is its simplicity: a mat, other people moving alongside you, a shared experience with room enough for your own mind to settle.

When the opportunity came to restore our 1920s building upstairs, right above Align Pilates, we knew it was exactly what we wanted. Real character, original details, and a space considered down to the last detail.

We took our time getting it right, because this is not just a business to us. It is personal, and we hope you can feel that the moment you walk through the door.

We would love for you to be part of it.
