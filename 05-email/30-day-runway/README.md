# 30 Day Runway email

- `email.html`: the finished email (600px, table-based, inline styles). Open it in a browser to preview.
- `images/`: every image the email uses, sized for email. `runway-graphic.jpg` is the promo graphic
  with the "DM us RUNWAY" lines cropped off.
- `preview.png`: full-length screenshot.

## Sending from Wix Email Marketing

Wix's email editor is drag-and-drop and does not accept pasted HTML, so rebuild it there top to bottom
using this file as the design and copy source:

1. Logo: `images/altitude-wordmark.png`
2. Image: `images/runway-graphic.jpg`
3. Text: eyebrow "30 Day Runway", heading "Your first 30 days are your runway", intro paragraph
4. Text between two divider lines: the "Join Unlimited, Gold, or Silver..." offer
5. Three columns: Unlimited $150, Gold $375, Silver $250
6. Text + button "Claim your refund", linked to `mailto:info@altitude.yoga?subject=RUNWAY`
7. Image: `images/warrior.jpg`
8. Dark section (background #272003): "Unlimited pays for itself in just 7 classes a month" + the four perks + three app screenshots
9. Two images: `images/class-stretch.jpg`, `images/forward-fold.jpg`
10. "Start your runway" + button "Book a class" linked to https://get.mndbdy.ly/mBLZj3Mfx7b
11. "How it works" fine print
12. Footer: address, Instagram, Facebook, info@altitude.yoga (Wix adds the unsubscribe link)

Colors: paper #EEECE6, stone #E2DFD7, ink #141414, muted #5D5B55, runway olive #272003, sage #B7BAAD.
Fonts: Playfair Display for headings, Helvetica for body, all caps for headings and labels.
