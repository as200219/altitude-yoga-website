# 30 Day Runway email

- `email.html`: the finished email (600px, table-based, inline styles). Open it in a browser to preview.
  The membership tiers match the Pricing page on altitude.yoga.
- `images/`: every image the email uses, sized for email. `runway-graphic.jpg` is the promo graphic
  with the "DM us RUNWAY" lines cropped off.
- `preview.png`: full-length screenshot.

## Sending from Wix Email Marketing

Paste the full contents of `email.html` into Wix's custom HTML option. The images are already
hosted online (on GitHub, linked to a fixed version of this repo), so they show up after pasting
with nothing to upload.

To swap a photo, upload the new one to Wix's media library and replace that image's `src="..."` address in the HTML.

Buttons: "Claim your refund" opens an email to info@altitude.yoga with the subject RUNWAY;
"Book a class" goes to https://get.mndbdy.ly/mBLZj3Mfx7b.

Colors: paper #EEECE6, stone #E2DFD7, ink #141414, muted #5D5B55, runway olive #272003, sage #B7BAAD.
Fonts: Playfair Display for headings, Helvetica for body, all caps for headings and labels.
