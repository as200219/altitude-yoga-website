How it works

Your 30 days start the day you take your first class. After you join, we'll refund every Altitude Yoga drop-in you attended in that window back to your card. Bought a class pack? The classes you've attended are refunded too, and any you haven't used stay on your pack to enjoy later. Late cancels, no-shows, and Align Pilates classes don't count. Already a member? If you joined within 30 days of your first class, you can still claim your refund. Refunds are up to $150. Memberships renew monthly, and you can cancel anytime. One per person.
