Frequently Asked Questions
What should I bring to class?
A mat and water.
What if I forget a mat?
We have complimentary loaner mats on hand.
Am I allowed to film or take photos in class?
Filming is never allowed except with explicit permission from studio staff. You're welcome to take photos before or after class, as long as no other participants are visible without their permission. During class, we ask that phones are stowed away.
What's your cancellation policy for drop-in classes?
Cancel more than 12 hours before class for a full refund, no fee. Cancellations within 12 hours of class are subject to a $12 fee. No-shows forfeit the full drop-in rate.
Do I have to commit to a long-term membership?
Memberships start with an initial 3-month commitment. After that, they continue month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice.
How do I cancel my membership?
Just email us and we'll send you a short cancellation form to complete. Your 30-day notice period begins once we receive your completed form.
What if I'm going on vacation or need a break?
You can freeze your membership instead of canceling, for up to 30 days per calendar year. Reach out to our team to set it up, and your billing will pause and pick back up automatically once your freeze ends.
What do I get with a membership?
Membership benefits vary by tier and may include buddy passes to Altitude Yoga, a discount on Manduka products, and access to member-exclusive events and classes. See our membership tiers for full details on what's included at each level.
If I buy a package at Align or Altitude, are they linked?
Yes. You can purchase a package from either studio, and it usually takes about 5 minutes to populate in the other studio's software before you can book. If you run into any issues, please contact us.
Is there an age requirement?
Yes, students must be 16 or older to attend regular classes (outside of designated kids' specialty classes). Minors must have a guardian attend their first class to sign a permission waiver. We reserve the right to revoke a minor's ability to attend class independently if their behavior becomes disruptive.
What should I know before taking a heated class?
Heated classes involve higher temperatures and increased sweat loss. We recommend consulting your physician before attending if you have high or low blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, a history of heat stroke, heat intolerance, or are pregnant. Hot yoga is not recommended for individuals with MS. Stay hydrated, listen to your body, and feel free to rest in child's pose at any time during class.
I'm running late — how late can I be?
We have a 5-minute grace period after class starts. After that, we're not able to let you into class, both to safely warm you up and out of respect for the instructor and other students already settling into practice.
Can I attend a class without a membership?
Yes, absolutely. We offer drop-ins, class packages, and memberships, so you can pick whatever works best for you. If you're not sure which option is the right fit, just ask — our client experience team is happy to help you figure out what makes the most sense for your schedule and goals.
How long are packages good for?
Drop-ins are valid for 30 days from purchase. 5-Class Packs last 3 months, and 10-Class Packs last 6 months.
