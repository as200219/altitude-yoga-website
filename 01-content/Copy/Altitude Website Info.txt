   * * Class Descriptions
   * EX: Class type- Description
   * Vinyasa - Link breath to movement by continuously moving from one posture to the next.
   * Power - A fitness-based style of vinyasa focused on building strength, flexibility, and endurance.
   * Yin “Descent” - Known to calm the nervous system and increase flexibility. Poses are held for extended periods of time to allow the connective tissue and fascia to release tension. 
   * “Climb & Descend” - The perfect blend of movement and stillness. Students will move through a slow vinyasa before slowing down with long, restorative holds.
   * Yoga Sculpt - This class blends the mindful movement of yoga with the strength and sculpting power of a full-body workout - yoga, dumbbell-based strength straining, and a stretch cooldown all in one class.
   * “Lift” - A structured, full-body strength class set to music, moving through segments that target legs, glutes, back, arms, and core - using dumbbells throughout. Each segment builds through steady, controlled movement to fatigue the muscle and keep your heart rate up. Expect a smooth, sweat-inducing format designed to build strength from head to toe.
