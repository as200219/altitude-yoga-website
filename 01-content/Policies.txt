Age Requirement
Students must be 16 or older to attend regular classes (outside of designated kids' specialty classes). Minors must have a guardian attend their first class to sign a permission waiver. We reserve the right to revoke a minor's ability to attend class independently if their behavior becomes disruptive. Exceptions to this policy may be considered on a case-by-case basis.
Heated Class Recommendations
Our heated classes involve higher temperatures and increased sweat loss. We recommend consulting your physician before attending if you have high or low blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, a history of heat stroke, heat intolerance, or are pregnant. Hot yoga is not recommended for individuals with MS. Please stay hydrated, listen to your body, and feel free to rest in child’s poise. 
Phone Policy
To keep our classes safe, focused, and distraction-free, we ask that you silence your phone and leave it in a locker or cubby before class begins. This helps everyone stay present and move safely.
If you have a medical reason to keep your phone with you, just let your instructor know before class begins — we’re happy to accommodate. 
Late Arrival
Our studio does not have a front desk, so for the safety and security of everyone inside, doors lock once class begins and late arrivals can't be admitted. Warming up properly is essential to preventing injury, and joining partway through also disrupts the focus of students already in class. Please arrive at least 10 minutes before class. Doors open 20 minutes before class starts.
