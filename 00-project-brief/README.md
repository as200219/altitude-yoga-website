# 00 - Project Brief

## Working goal

Create a welcoming, clear webpage that introduces the yoga studio's teachers and helps visitors decide which teacher, class, or next step is right for them.

## Questions to answer together

- What should the page be called?
- Who is the primary audience: new students, regular students, or both?
- Should the page represent the whole studio or sit inside a larger website section?
- What should the main action be: book a class, view the schedule, contact the studio, or meet the teachers?
- How many teachers should appear in the first version?
- Do teacher cards need filters such as style, level, or class time?
- What tone should the writing have: calm, bright, grounded, playful, elegant, or something else?

## First version scope

- Responsive teacher directory.
- Teacher photo, name, role, short introduction, and specialties.
- Optional link or button for classes, booking, or a full bio.
- Studio-level introduction and a clear call to action.
- Accessible structure that can be recreated in Wix.

## Out of scope for the scaffold

- Final brand colors, fonts, photography, and copy.
- Booking or payment functionality.
- CMS or database integration.
- Final Wix styling.
